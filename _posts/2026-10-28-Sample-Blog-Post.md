# Starting the Journey: Learning, Building, and Becoming a Better Engineer

*October 2026 · 5 min read*

## A Beginning

Every journey has a starting point. Sometimes it's a big decision, sometimes it's a small step, and sometimes it's simply the realization that you want to do more with what you already know.

For me, this is one of those steps.

I'm Thomas, a Software Engineering student with a growing interest in how software works, how complex systems are built, and how technology can be used to solve real problems.

I've spent time learning programming, working on projects, experimenting with tools, and exploring different areas of technology. But I've also realized something: **learning to write code is only one part of becoming a good software engineer.**

There's a much bigger world behind the code.

And that's what I want to explore.

## Beyond Writing Code

It's easy to focus on learning a programming language, discovering a new framework, or getting an application to work. Those are important milestones, especially when you're starting out.

But I've become increasingly interested in the questions that come after the code works.

Why was a particular architecture chosen? How do databases, APIs, and frontend applications fit together? What makes software maintainable? How do operating systems manage resources? What happens behind the scenes when one computer communicates with another?

These are the kinds of questions I want to understand more deeply.

My goal isn't just to become someone who can build applications. I want to develop the fundamentals, problem-solving skills, and engineering judgment needed to build software that is reliable, understandable, and useful.

I know that takes time, practice, and plenty of mistakes along the way.

That's part of the process.

## Learning by Building

One of the most valuable experiences I've had so far is working on a Stock Exchange Website as part of a university team project.
